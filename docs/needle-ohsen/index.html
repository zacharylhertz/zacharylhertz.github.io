Method note

On this page, I show model estimates of win likelihood. This is not an official result. The model is a county-level estimate of how many votes remain and how they might split. I aggregate this up to a statewide estimate.

I start with county reports of absentee ballots from mail and early in-person vote. These historically lean Democratic, so early counts tend to lean towards Democrats. The model considers early and Election Day votes separately, and starts with a prior that Election Day votes will lean Republican. Before votes are counted, the estimate works off the polling average and the number of absentee ballots received by each county. For a benchmark, I model how each county would vote in a tied race. This starts with the 2024 Senate results at the county level, split into Election Day and early votes, and applies a uniform swing. Counted votes are compared by type, so early vote is compared to early vote benchmarks. Groups show as ‘too early’ until at least 10 percent of expected votes have been counted, and as running ahead of benchmarks only when performances exceed 3 point differences overall.

Results are unofficial vote counts, from the Ohio Secretary of State. The page checks for new data every minute, but the data file may lag by a few minutes.
