Method note

On this page, I model the final result for CA-11. This is not an official result. Margin is one candidate’s share of the two-candidate vote minus the other’s, in percentage points.

San Francisco counts in batches, slowly. The first ballots counted are mail ballots that arrived early and ballots cast in-person on Election Day. Ballots arriving later are counted in daily releases, and may split unpredictably. The model attempts to account for this by projecting how uncounted ballots might shift the margin, with narrowing uncertainty as the number of projected outstanding votes decreases.

The page checks for new data every minute, but may be constrained by the pace at which the Department of Elections releases new results.
